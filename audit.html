The audit

Websites, print, and the systems behind them, checked before your customers find the flaws. One clear report on a set timeline. It is the easiest way to see how we work before you commit to anything larger.

The cost of a mistake

A typo printed ten thousand times. A form that screen-reader users can’t finish. A launch you have to walk back in public.

The price of peace of mind

A price agreed up front, and every flaw caught before your customers see it.

What we audit

  • Digital. Interface and usability, accessibility against WCAG 2.1 AA (checked by hand, not just by a scanner), and QA in current Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge, on desktop and mobile.
  • Print and pre-production. Proofs, prepress, type, color, copy, and postal requirements for direct mail, checked before anything goes to press.
  • Systems and service. We play both your customer and your employee, walk every step of every stream, and find and relieve the pain points, for ease, speed, and clarity.
  • Copy. A line-by-line copyedit for clarity, consistency, and tone, to AP style or your house style.

What you get

  • A prioritized report with the fix for each finding.
  • A walkthrough with your team to go through every finding.
  • The fee is set before we start, based on what we are auditing.
  • It comes off your first invoice if you continue with us within 30 days.
  • The report is yours to keep, whatever you decide.
