Who you’ll work with

Kevin Broadie

Founder & principal consultant

I have been a designer, strategist, and technologist for my entire 25-year career, and I have run KBCS since 2004. I have led visual communications for government agencies, built hospitality brands, migrated retail operations to new tech stacks, and managed IT for an art museum. I started in a newsroom, editing copy to the AP Stylebook, and careful copyediting has been part of my work ever since.

I believe that communication is an ethical practice and that accessibility is a design principle, not a compliance exercise. I won’t tear down a wall until I understand why it was built.

I lead every engagement and stay your single point of contact. When part of the job calls for someone else, such as a developer, a photographer, or a printer, I bring in the best person I know for it and stay accountable for the result.
