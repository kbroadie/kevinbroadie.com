Public sector · CVAG

$50 million for the ACCESS Indian Canyon Drive project

Brief
Storms kept closing Indian Canyon Drive at its low-water crossings, cutting a main route to the region’s only Level 1 trauma center. The Coachella Valley Association of Governments (CVAG) needed state funding to build bridges and keep the road open.
Did
As CVAG’s in-house designer, co-facilitated the grant writing for the state’s new Local Transportation Climate Adaptation Program and moved the team onto a shared, collaborative workflow.
Result
A $50 million award, approved unanimously by the California Transportation Commission, and one of only two projects in the state to receive the highest priority ranking.
