Public sector · Regional government

A $50 million grant, ranked first in California

Brief
Competitive state and federal transportation grant applications, written against fixed deadlines.
Did
Co-facilitated the grant writing and moved the team onto a shared, collaborative workflow. When one federal grant program was replaced mid-cycle with a new RFP and the same deadline, that workflow let the team retarget in time.
Result
A $50 million transportation grant, the highest-ranked application in California.
