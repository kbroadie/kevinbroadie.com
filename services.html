The work

What happens when you bring us in

We refine the design and harden the technology behind it. Here is how the work moves.

  1. 01

    Audit

    We find the flaws before your customers do

    A hospitality rebrand or a public-sector portal lands on our desk, and we pressure-test it across brand, copy, interface, and the process behind it, playing both your customer and your employee, before anyone outside ever sees it.

  2. 02

    Refine

    We take good design and make it great

    A real-estate pitch or an arts and culture identity arrives close to right. We sharpen the copy, type, layout, and system until the work earns its confidence and nothing is wasted.

  3. 03

    Direct

    We give a brand a direction that holds

    Strategy and art direction for a private club or a lifestyle label, carried across print, digital, and email, and built to hold up under scrutiny long after launch.

  4. 04

    Build

    We build interfaces people can use

    A site for a nonprofit or a tool for a media team, engineered on the front end with accessibility designed in from the first decision rather than bolted on at the end.

  5. 05

    Automate

    We build the tool when none exists

    When a retail operation outgrows its spreadsheets, we turn the manual work into dashboards and automation that run quietly in the background.

Alongside the work

The parts that happen in the room

  • Train

    Staff who own the new way of working

    Hands-on training on the tools and workflows we put in place, documented so it sticks after we leave. Kevin has taught digital-skills courses at a community college and trained staff through every system change he has led.

  • Present

    Presentations built for the room

    Public presentations, executive briefings, and community outreach, written and designed for the audience, the setting, and the decision in front of them.

  • Advise

    A sounding board

    Someone who has seen the problem before. Bring the half-formed idea, the hard decision, or the need for a second opinion, and leave with a clearer next step.
